FAQs

Who is this frame tool for?

Anyone heading to Hacker House Goa 2026 — hackers, designers, founders, mentors, or just fans who want a branded profile picture or badge.

Is it free to use?

Yes. Generate, download, and share as many frames or builder IDs as you want. No account required.

Can I use this if I am not attending?

Sure — spread the vibe. Just keep the #FrameInGoa tag honest and point people to hhgoa.com for tickets.

What image formats work?

JPG, PNG, and HEIC/HEIF are supported. Portrait or landscape photos both work; the tool cover-fits them automatically.

How do I share with the preview image?

Hit “Share to X”. The tool uploads your PNG and returns a link that shows your frame as the social-card preview.
